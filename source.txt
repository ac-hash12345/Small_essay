一、小作文

第一篇：小作文 - 建议信（P.48 经典范文004）

中英逐句对照版

第一段：表示欢迎

Dear Friends,

亲爱的朋友们：

① On behalf of the Students' Union of Beijing Foreign Studies University, I would like to extend our heartfelt welcome to all the overseas students who will study in our university.

我谨代表北京外国语大学学生会，向来我校学习的留学生表示衷心的欢迎。

第二段：提出建议

② It is my great pleasure to offer you several practical suggestions.

我很荣幸能给你们提一些实用的建议。

③ In the first place, life in this prestigious university is perhaps not as easy as you have expected.

首先，这所知名学府的生活也许并不像你们所设想的那么轻松。

④ You may be tightly bound by daily classes, abundant homework and frequent exams.

你们可能会面临每日的课程、大量的作业和频繁的考试。

⑤ Thus, be prepared for the hectic life here.

因此，做好准备迎接这里的繁忙生活吧。

⑥ In the second place, in spite of all those burdens, various extracurricular activities such as sports meets, speech contests and social gatherings will still provide ample opportunities to make friends.

其次，尽管有以上负担，运动会、演讲比赛以及联谊会等各种各样的课外活动仍会提供大量结交朋友的机会。

⑦ So, enjoy your life in this esteemed institute!

所以，尽情享受这所著名大学里的生活吧！

第三段：总结与期望

⑧ All in all, although there is a lot of pressure, the time you spend on our campus will be worthwhile and enhance your whole life.

总之，尽管有很多压力，但你们在这所校园里度过的时光将意义非凡，并为你的整个人生增添光彩。

⑨ Hope you like it here!

希望你们喜欢这里！

Yours sincerely,

Li Ming

您真诚的，

李明

第二篇：小作文 - 介绍+邀请信（P.68 经典范文010）

中英逐句对照版

第一段：写作目的

Dear John,

亲爱的约翰：

① As you know, I am extremely keen on the ancient Chinese novel—Romance of the Three Kingdoms.

如你所知，我非常喜欢《三国演义》这部中国古代小说。

② Now I am planning to direct a short play revised from it.

现在我计划执导一部根据它改编的短剧。

第二段：介绍短剧

③ Romance of the Three Kingdoms, as one of the Four Great Classical Novels in China, mainly depicts the political and military struggles during the Three Kingdoms Period and reveals the turbulent society.

《三国演义》作为中国四大名著之一，主要描写了三国时期的政治和军事斗争，揭示了当时动荡的社会。

④ It successfully portrays images of classic characters, such as Zhuge Liang, Liu Bei, Guan Yu, Cao Cao, and so on.

它成功地塑造了诸葛亮、刘备、关羽、曹操等经典人物形象。

第三段：邀请参演

⑤ Since you excel in acting, I cordially invite you to participate in the play and act as the leading hero, Zhuge Liang.

由于你擅长表演，我诚挚地邀请你参演这部剧，扮演主人公诸葛亮。

⑥ I'm sure you will be quite interested in it.

我相信你会非常感兴趣的。

⑦ Looking forward to your reply.

期待你的回信。

Yours,

Li Ming

你的，

李明

第三篇：小作文 - 介绍/推荐信（P.70 经典范文011）

中英逐句对照版

Dear friends,

亲爱的朋友们：

① I am writing the email to recommend one of the most prestigious Chinese celebrities to you, Confucius.

我写这封邮件是为了向你们介绍中国最知名的名人之一：孔子。

② First of all, being a great thinker, educator and founder of Confucianism, Confucius is an ancient sage during the Spring and Autumn Period.

首先，孔子是一位大思想家、大教育家、儒家学派创始人、春秋时期的一位古代圣人。

③ In addition, his words and life story were recorded by his disciples and their students in The Analects.

其次，他的言论和生平活动记录在由他的弟子和再传弟子编成的《论语》一书中。

④ As an enduring classic of ancient Chinese culture, The Analects has influenced all thinkers, writers and politicians in the over-2000-year-long history of China after Confucius.

《论语》是中国古代文化的经典著作。在孔子以后两千多年的中国历史上，没有哪一位思想家、文学家、政治家不受《论语》的影响。

⑤ Finally, much of Confucian thought still remains the interest of not only the Chinese people but also people in other parts of the world.

最后，孔子的很多思想不仅仍然受到中国人的重视，而且受到整个国际社会的重视。

⑥ If you have any questions about Confucius, please feel free to contact me.

如果您对孔子有任何想了解的，请随时与我联系。

Yours sincerely,

Li Ming

您真诚的，

李明

第四篇：小作文 - 投诉信（P.73 经典范文013）

中英逐句对照版

Dear Sir or Madam,

尊敬的先生或女士：

As a regular passenger of your train, I am extremely disappointed to find that the service provided recently has been far from satisfactory.

作为贵列车的一位常客，我发现贵方近期提供的服务远远不能令人满意，对此我深感失望。

Therefore, I am writing this letter to draw your attention to these issues in the hope that the present situation can be improved.

因此，我写这封信是想引起您对这些问题的关注，希望能够改进现状。

As we all know, punctuality is essential to the railway service.

众所周知，准时对于铁路服务非常重要。

However, the train has been delayed frequently, which has brought me much trouble and inconvenience.

然而，这趟列车经常晚点，给我带来很多的麻烦和不便。

Even worse are the poor attitude of some of your on-board staff.

更糟糕的是，这趟列车部分乘务员的服务态度欠佳。

Their service has not embodied the principle, "The customer should come first."

他们的服务并未体现“顾客至上”的原则。

I believe you will take this matter into serious consideration and provide a satisfactory reply as soon as possible.

我相信您会认真考虑这个问题，并尽快给出满意的答复。

At the same time, I sincerely hope that you will review your management system.

同时，我真诚地希望您能审查管理系统。

I would be glad to see improvements in your railway service.

我将很高兴看到贵方列车服务的改进。

Yours sincerely,

Li Ming

您真诚的，

李明
